Terminology“AES” is BlockSign’s product label for the standard signing path. In formal eIDAS English, “advanced electronic signature” corresponds to the German term FES.
Conformity domain

eIDAS or ZertES

EU / EEA

eIDAS

The European trust-services framework. A FES or QES is issued under the eIDAS provider profile when the signer’s verified identity and selected journey support that regime.

Switzerland

ZertES

The Swiss electronic-signature framework. For this reviewer journey, the ZertES option is the Switzerland-specific provider path.

A single FES or QES is produced under one concrete legal regime. By default, the appropriate regime can be resolved per signer from verified provider capabilities. This also allows different signers on the same document to use their valid eIDAS or ZertES profile, unless the document requires one common regime.

Legal boundaryThe signature regime is not selected only from a passport label. Governing law, transaction type, contractual form requirements and the receiving authority’s rules still determine which form is required. This guide is not legal advice.
Reviewer access

How to use the reviewer voucher

1
Sign in to the reviewer account

Open the BlockSign dashboard with the account for which the voucher was issued.

2
Select FES or QES readiness

Open the signature-level or profile readiness area and choose the required level and available legal regime.

3
Open the corresponding checkout

The checkout creates the concrete level-upgrade or document-payment target.

4
Choose Voucher

Enter the reviewer voucher code and apply it to that checkout.

5
Continue with a matching document

The account now shows paid/onboarding pending. Upload or open a document that requires the purchased level and regime; Swisscom onboarding begins only when the signer starts that document’s signature.

What the voucher does

It settles an eligible pending upgrade or document checkout and activates the corresponding BlockSign entitlement.

What the voucher does not do

It is not a certificate, identity proof, provider credential or substitute for Swisscom/NextID KYC.

Person-bound readiness
The verified identity, mobile number and reusable provider reference are associated with the person’s account.
1
Authenticate the mobile number

QES uses the Mobile ID journey. The current FES MAB journey starts with PWDOTP and may fall back to Mobile ID when Swisscom considers suitable provider evidence available.

2
Complete the level-specific KYC

FES verifies first and last name. QES additionally verifies country, address, place of birth and date of birth. Swisscom or NextID performs the check.

3
Bind the verified identity

After successful review, the provider result is linked to the signer’s account and legal-regime profile.

4
Create reusable provider readiness

The person-bound Swisscom identity or Fast Track reference is valid for 365 days. It may shorten later eligible journeys, but each document still requires fresh provider authorisation. The personal signing certificate evidence is issued on demand for the concrete signature.

Why there is no onboarding recordingThere is no synthetic Swisscom sandbox for this reviewer flow. Recording the production process would expose a real person’s protected identity information and reproduce provider-owned regulated screens that BlockSign is not authorised to publish.
Concrete signature request

From document to Swisscom signature

1
Upload and prepare the PDF

The sender selects FES or QES, chooses the available legal regime, adds the signer and places one signature field for that person.

2
Open the signing invitation

The signer reviews the document and starts the signature using the BlockSign account connected to the enrolled identity.

3
Complete optional biometric MFA

If biometric MFA is configured and enabled in the BlockSign account, it is requested immediately before BlockSign transmits the signature request to Swisscom.

4
Prepare the cryptographic request

BlockSign pre-signs the PDF and transmits the document digest, the configured Swisscom credential ID and the required signature context. A reusable provider reference may be supplied when it is still valid.

5
Give the final declaration of intent

Swisscom prompts the signer through Mobile ID. The signer explicitly approves the concrete signature request directly toward Swisscom as the QTSP.

6
Embed and complete

Swisscom creates the on-demand signer certificate evidence and returns the QTSP-backed CMS signature and validation material. BlockSign embeds the result in the prepared PDF and completes the document workflow.

Two distinct controlsOptional BlockSign biometric MFA confirms an additional local authentication factor before handoff. It never replaces Swisscom’s provider-controlled authorisation: QES requires the final Mobile ID declaration of intent, while FES follows its PWDOTP/provider-selected authentication path.

After signing

Completion and verification

1

Download

The authorised sender and signer can access the completed PDF from the BlockSign dashboard after the workflow is complete.

2

Upload for verification

Open the BlockSign verification page and upload exactly one signed PDF.

3

Review evidence

Review cryptographic integrity, detected certificate/signature classification and the legal-regime information available in the PDF.

Verification boundaryA technically valid PDF signature and certificate classification provide important evidence. They do not by themselves decide whether the transaction satisfies every local legal, contractual or authority-specific form requirement.
Reviewer test sequence

Compact walkthrough checklist

Voucher and document

  • Sign in with the reviewer account
  • Select QES and the available eIDAS or ZertES entitlement
  • Open checkout and apply the reviewer voucher
  • Confirm paid/onboarding pending
  • Upload or open a matching non-sensitive sample PDF

Start from signing

  • Add one QES field for the reviewer
  • Open the signing invitation
  • Start the document signature
  • Continue into the Swisscom provider journey

Provider enrolment and consent

  • Authenticate with Mobile ID for QES
  • Complete Swisscom/NextID QES KYC
  • Wait for identity approval and reusable provider readiness
  • Complete optional BlockSign biometrics if enabled
  • Approve the final Swisscom Mobile ID prompt

Result

  • Return to BlockSign after provider consent
  • Wait for signature completion
  • Download the completed PDF
  • Upload it on the verification page
  • Review integrity and certificate evidence
Recommended test documentUse a non-sensitive sample PDF. The real identity data needed for Swisscom onboarding should never be included in reviewer reports, screenshots or recordings.
Final clarification

What the written walkthrough replaces

This guide provides the operational information that a video would normally demonstrate while respecting the identity provider’s process ownership and the signer’s privacy. BlockSign can explain the handoff, expected data categories, reusable identity-reference lifecycle, on-demand certificate evidence, consent sequence and returned result. It cannot publish or simulate provider screens that are unavailable in a sandbox and contain a real person’s protected identity data.

No bypass for reviewersThe reviewer voucher removes the eligible payment barrier only. All normal provider controls continue to apply: PWDOTP or provider-selected authentication for FES, and KYC plus the final Mobile ID declaration of intent for QES.
